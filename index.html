Find an Issue

Got Questions?

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about joining and contributing to OpenLake.

I'm a first-year with no coding background. Can I join?

Absolutely! OpenLake is designed to take you from scratch. Workshops, pair programming, and hackathons like HelloWorld are built specifically for first-year beginners.

Do I need permission from seniors to build a project?

No! OpenLake promotes a flat, decentralized culture. Anyone can pitch an idea, open a pull request, or form a project team.

What languages and stacks are used?

We maintain repositories in Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, React, Rust, C/C++, Docker, Go, and Shell scripts.

How do I get official contributor access?

Once your first 2 PRs are merged into any official OpenLake repository, you automatically receive contributor status in the GitHub organization!
